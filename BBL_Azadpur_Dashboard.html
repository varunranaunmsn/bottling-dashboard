The 90-Day Return Lag
Critical Procurement Cycle
Bottles dispatched in April return in July. High-demand May–July creates Aug–Oct procurement shortfalls. Pre-stock 200K units in Jan–Mar to prevent forced new-bottle purchases at ₹17.9–₹20.7.
Production Loss Rate (P-Loss)
~2.8% of Production
Bottles rejected at filling/QC. Miller: P-Loss = 79% of its total losses — fix the filling line. TB: demand volume = 50% of its losses — control breakage at scale.

01
MB
Critical
Fix Miller Filling Line P-Loss Rate
Miller's production loss drives 79% of its ₹80.7L total losses (RF importance: 0.794). Miller's 410g bottle needs dedicated filling-valve calibration separate from TB's 425g profile. Install fill-level sensors, recalibrate for Miller's bottle geometry, implement daily rejection monitoring. Target: P-Loss rate from 2.8% → 2.4%.
Estimated annual saving₹4–7 L / yr
02
TB
Critical
Reduce TB Breakage 20% in Peak Months
TB breakage = 1.24M bottles over 35 months = 59% of TB yard loss. May–July accounts for ~45% of annual breakage. Install anti-vibration conveyor cushioning, reduce line speed 5% in peak months, add visual inspection at top 3 transfer points. 20% reduction = 248K fewer broken TB bottles per year.
Estimated annual saving₹9–14 L / yr
03
TBMB
High Priority
Pre-Season Old Bottle Buffer Stock
The 90-day return lag creates a predictable June–August procurement gap every year. Buy 150K TB + 50K Miller old bottles in January–March at ₹5 each (₹10L investment). This replaces June–August forced new-bottle purchases at ₹17.9–₹20.7. Net saving on 200K bottles: ₹26–30L over the gap period.
Estimated annual saving₹8–12 L / yr
04
MB
High Priority
Increase MB Old Bottle Mix to 65%+
Miller saves ₹15.70 per old bottle (vs ₹12.90 for TB). Each 1pp improvement in Miller old mix saves ~₹35K/month. Punjab already achieves 82% Miller recovery in FY26 — use as template for Chandigarh and Haryana. Negotiate Miller-specific collection incentives with distributors.
Estimated annual saving at 65%₹5–8 L / yr
05
TBMB
High Priority
Deposit Scheme for Pondicherry & Maharashtra
Both states show 0% recovery for both brands — every bottle dispatched is written off at full new-bottle cost. Implement ₹10–15 refundable deposit per bottle, or restrict dispatch volumes to historical recovery rates. Alternatively, partner with local glass aggregators for minimum cullet recovery (₹2.5–3/bottle).
Estimated annual saving₹4–8 L / yr
06
TBMB
Medium Priority
Deploy ML Predictive Procurement Dashboard
The RF model (R²=0.956) accurately predicts monthly losses. Use it to: (a) plan old-bottle pre-purchases 90 days ahead from demand forecasts, (b) flag months when predicted return rate drops below 50% for emergency procurement action, (c) monitor Miller filling-line yield weekly to catch P-Loss spikes early.
